Contract: verify signed records/device membership and current account lifetime;
recheck authorization after asynchronous work. Local biometric/unlock adapters are
phone-specific; hosted credentials/tokens are not a second identity protocol.
Protected owner binding, editable operational notes, and contact/group permissions
are separate concepts. Notes and AI-generated text cannot grant permission.
Do not infer live enforcement settings from local code or from a saved nickname.

State authority: account controller/vault, verified identity records, durable
approval decisions. UI state and AI memory may describe them but cannot override.
Failures: locked account, expired/revoked device, mismatched owner binding, stale
approval evidence, blocked peer. Do not flatten all of these into offline.
