Android Home-button call window — October 12, 2026

The migrated call lifecycle never called the existing native
MyChatService.setPictureInPictureEnabled method. The native Activity already
supports Android Picture-in-Picture, including auto-enter on Android 12+ and
onUserLeaveHint on Android 8–11.

rootCallLifetime now disables PiP at lease startup, enables it only when the
same call is both connected and its foreground service is ready, and disables
it before stopping that service. Serialized transitions protect against a late
startup or a connection callback arming an obsolete call. Connected voice and
video calls use this window.

RootAccountCallScreen keeps the WebRTC owner mounted across Home/return.
On Android background transition it hides the separate call Modal and renders
remote video (or call identity/timer for voice) on the Activity surface captured
by system PiP. Returning restores the preceding full or in-app minimized view.
