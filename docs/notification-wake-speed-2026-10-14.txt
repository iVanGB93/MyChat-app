Shared scheduler change: prioritize unattempted newest deposits over old due
work for up to three turns; every fourth selection serves the oldest eligible
envelope. Preserve one selection per second, signature verification, durable
custody, expiry, provider deduplication, and exponential retry backoff. This
changes local ordering, not peer advertisements or request rate. Continuous
fresh arrivals cannot monopolize all scheduler turns.
