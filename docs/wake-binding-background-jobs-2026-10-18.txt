  A: foreground SUCCEEDED in 162 ms; background SUCCEEDED in 10,036 ms.

- Ciphertext signatures/digests do not bind optional wakeMode/wakeSignature.
  Inline-envelope admission now additionally requires exact agreement with the
  verified signed wake. Inconsistent inline metadata is not admitted; the valid
