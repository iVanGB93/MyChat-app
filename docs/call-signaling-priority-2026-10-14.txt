Bounded direct call signaling priority — 2026-10-14

Change in persistentAxon.ts
Direct call-control and signed media signaling previously shared the two custody
reservations and immediately returned null/false whenever push was waiting.
They now have two independent urgent reservations, still using the single
authenticated request/response exchange. They wait for active exchange/auth to
finish; there is no preemption and no extra parallel wire exchange.
After two call exchanges, a waiting background reservation takes a turn. Recovery,
directory, push and call-relay polling honor this bounded priority. Attachment
requests also yield while call reservations are outstanding, as they previously
yielded to the custody reservation used by calls. Existing verification, queue
timeouts, receipts, rate limits and authentication renewal remain unchanged.

Evidence
Real signed paired-session regression holds a custody request open, queues another
recovery request and push registration, then queues two call requests. A third
call is rejected by the cap. After releasing the active request, both calls are
acknowledged before queued background work, which also completes. No disconnect.
