LAN stability investigation, October 20, 2026

Production phone logs confirmed repeated LAN exchange-timeout:custody and
chat-message-rejected:unexpected-ack closures. RTC fallback also repeatedly
failed to open. These are real session closures, not just a presence indicator.

Changes:
- Normal encrypted chat remembers at most 64 expired packet ID/digest pairs for
  60 seconds. A cryptographically validated late ACK matching one exact packet
  is consumed without closing the link or acknowledging a newer retry.
  Authentication, encryption, tamper checks and replay checks remain in place.
- Authenticated custody exchanges receive up to 15 seconds, bounded by identity
  proof expiry, rather than tearing down the link after four seconds of durable
  storage delay. This is a stability mitigation, not a storage-speed fix.

Regression checks cover late ACK versus a newer same-ID retry, forged ciphertext,
replay rejection, slow custody response, and bounded timeout under suspended JS
timers. Full npm run check passed. Live emulator texts/read receipts and generated
file checksum checks passed; logs in builds/lan-stability-emulators.txt and
builds/lan-130kb-emulators.txt. Emulator timing is not a phone LAN benchmark.
