Recovery pacing and reconnect validation — October 13, 2026 (America/New_York)

Changes
- Custody recovery no longer immediately repolls after an unusable packet. Packet failures back off 15, 30, 60, 120, 240 then 300 seconds, bounded in memory. Receipt/envelope validation and durable delivery acknowledgements are unchanged.
- Normal and group mailbox polls share a local per-identity token budget across sockets/reconnects: default 60/minute, burst 4. Deposits and acknowledgements do not spend poll credits.
- Authenticated custody work exceeding its configurable soft allowance returns retry with a bounded retry interval; a separate 4096-frame/minute hard ceiling retains flood protection. General control, unauthenticated and media protections remain separate.
- Couriers honor retry intervals and measure normal retry waits from completion, avoiding compressed retries after slow I/O.
- Successful custody handoffs in mobile and hosted own-chat outboxes wait 60 seconds before rechecking. A recipient becoming directly connected permits prompt direct delivery; custody acceptance never means recipient delivery.
- Network > Advanced diagnostics includes adjustable recovery checks (30–90/minute) and recovery traffic allowance (512–2048/minute), plus Restore recovery defaults. Preferences persist and apply immediately; resetting preferences does not erase messages or clear rate counters. It cannot change a remote peer's limits. Installing this release is required once before these controls exist in the official app.
