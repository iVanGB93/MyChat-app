Android video-call screen sharing — October 16, 2026

Options > Share screen replaces the existing video sender with a display-capture
track. The microphone, call transport and original camera track remain call-owned.
The camera is disabled during sharing and returns to its preceding enabled/off
state on Stop share. Flip is hidden during sharing. The preview is unmirrored;
the remote video uses contain so a shared display is not cropped.

Scope: entire-display capture with microphone audio; no system-audio capture or
simultaneous camera-plus-screen stream. Android requests session consent. Native
MediaProjection foreground service permission, WebRTC startup configuration and
notification resources are preserved by withForegroundService. The version-guarded
postinstall patch requests default-display capture on Android 14+ and uses the
older permission intent on earlier Android. Single-app capture produced no frames
in the phone test and is outside this initial version; the final system prompt
defaults to the entire display. Older clients cannot start capture until updated.
