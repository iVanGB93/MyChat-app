Audit fixes — 2026-10-15

Notification recovery
Removed the per-message FIFO wait. Concurrent verified hints now share a single
300 ms network pump, while retaining separate bounded receipt-completion waits.
Each runtime lease is released independently; native wake is released only when
the last recovery ends. New inline content is admitted without waiting for an
unrelated missing receipt. Expiry, locked-account and wrong-account checks remain.
No delivery receipt is inferred merely from notification display or persistence.
Tests cover duplicate bursts, later inline payloads, expiry, errors, transition to
background, and two unresolved hints completing in one approximately 20-second
window instead of approximately 40 seconds. This is deterministic test evidence,
not a fresh real-world FCM/cold-start latency measurement.

LAN media
Enabled a maximum of two large encrypted batches in flight. Large-batch prefetch
remains bounded to two batches; small RTC windows retain their negotiated limits.
Failure tests prove only the verified contiguous prefix is checkpointed and all
requests are drained. Cryptographic verification and final checksum are unchanged.
Both Dev phones restarted to ensure their workers loaded the new implementation.
Two final 5 MiB transfers completed in 13.188 and 12.523 seconds at the sender;
both receiver SHA-256 checks matched. Earlier serial observations were 13.805 and
13.950 seconds. Sample size is small; no general throughput guarantee is claimed.
The remaining transfer time is still too high for a fast LAN and needs profiling.
