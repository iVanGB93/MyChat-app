AXONIC MIGRATION GAP FIXES — OCTOBER 11, 2026

Implemented
Group media and voice replies: reply metadata is carried through group audience snapshots, file/media review, persisted selections, voice enqueue, attachment preparation, ledger admission and encrypted group envelopes. Group quotes support third-member authors. Outgoing references must identify an available original in that group; deleted, missing and wrong-author originals are rejected. Receive validation allows the optional reply field with exact author/id schema, while preserving ordinary attachment envelopes and immutable replay checks. Reply state clears only after a successful queued send; picker cancellation keeps it.
Group message info: new GroupMessageInfo route and screen, accessible from the group message menu. Shows sender, original creation time, and each original recipient's pending/network-stored/delivered/read status plus totals. Recipients come from delivery rows rather than current group membership. Incoming messages do not fabricate per-recipient status or timestamps.
Message sounds: restored the original message_sent.wav and message_received.wav clips. New outgoing text/sticker messages emit feedback after persistence; media/voice batches emit once after staging. Incoming messages emit feedback only on fresh durable admission to the visible chat. Group fan-out, receipt updates, retries, duplicate deliveries and history loads do not emit extra tones. Local mute, notification sound preference, silent/vibrate mode, app/account changes, recording and active calls are respected. Players and listeners release on completion/cleanup; stopped or background events cannot play later on resume.
