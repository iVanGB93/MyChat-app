Direct stream waiting and startup recovery — 2026-10-14

Changed rootAttachmentStreams and directAttachmentStream:
- A pump waiting for remote progress now wakes after processing incoming frames
  instead of repeatedly querying the durable lease on zero-delay timers.
- Silent channels still revalidate the lease every 250 ms; close/cancel wakes the
  waiter. Active send bursts retain their UI yield. Native admission, credit,
  checksum verification and signed receipt behavior remain unchanged.
- A thrown startup request before any media lane exists now returns unavailable,
  allowing the worker's short readiness retry. Failures after lane creation still
  return false and retain the long failed-stream cooldown and encrypted fallback.
