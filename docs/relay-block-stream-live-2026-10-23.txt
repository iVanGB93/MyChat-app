Timing contract / limitations
Upload timer: enqueue invocation through observed complete relay custody; includes
preparation, encryption, scheduling, upload, persistence and polling, but excludes
synthetic fixture creation. Download timer: receiver launch through observed
verified file; includes startup, reconnect, discovery, download, decryption,
publication, polling and the diagnostic file-hash check. The deliberate offline
gap and subsequent sender restart/receipt check are not in either timer.
These are functional diagnostic observations, not raw network throughput, paired
before/after results or statistically established speedups. The 3 MB encrypted
block still materializes for existing verification/storage. Performance remains
slow and needs further profiling; no speed improvement is claimed here.
