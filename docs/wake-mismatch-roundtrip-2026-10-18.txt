Fix implemented
The mobile report previously discarded scheduler.pendingSample. A cumulative
mismatch counter alone could not say when the failure occurred or whether a
retained item was still retrying. Reports now include at most eight private and
eight group observations: request count, fixed last-outcome code, last-observed
timestamp and next-retry eligibility timestamp (or null for no eligible gateway).
Projection drops all IDs, content, secrets and arbitrary reason text; malformed
numbers/rows cannot bypass the allowlist. No stable aliases or cross-report row
correlation are claimed. 'validation' is a nonspecific scheduler state, not proof
of successful validation. Retry time is eligibility, not a delivery promise.
This uses existing observations; no new polling, background work or storage.
