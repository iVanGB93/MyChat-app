Receipt and call-delay follow-up, October 20, 2026
Scope: emulator-5554 and emulator-5556, com.axonic.dev only. Phones untouched.

Confirmed code defects:
1. Direct outbox timeout discarded a later successful transport acknowledgment.
   Timeout still starts custody fallback. A late success can now settle only the
   same pending raw message under the original active account/runtime generation,
   after rechecking blocking/cancellation. No timer or custody acceptance is
   considered delivery. Failed late persistence leaves retry/recovery available.
2. Non-direct call signals depended on regular relay polling. Saved call events
   now immediately flush to eligible relays when no direct peer is available, or
   after the direct attempt fails. Only signed, persisted, still-pending events
   are sent. Relay deposit is never a recipient receipt. Terminal cancellation
   has its own bounded slot so a stalled invitation does not hold it up.

Regression checks: late success, stop/account switch/block/network cancellation;
new invitation during stalled inbox polling; cancellation during stalled invite.

Emulator normal-flow observations (coarse debugger polling, not precise timings):
Before: text delivery .354/.287s, reads .231/.222s, 18944-byte file 1.821s correct.
After: text delivery .285/1.807s, reads .360/.261s, file 1.841s correct.
Calls: before incoming 2.219s/cancel .228s; after incoming 2.196s/cancel 1.742s.
These samples establish functionality, not a normal-path speedup. Deterministic
stalled-operation tests establish bypass of the identified waits. Production
