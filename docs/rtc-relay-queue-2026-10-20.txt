signal while two signed identity lookups were running. Persistent per-socket
serialization alone cannot avoid this shared relay bottleneck across axons.

The router now permits two active lookups and four queued packets. It checks
frame size and connected authenticated source before queueing, rechecks source
expiry/connection and generation on execution, and cancels queued work on clear.
Existing signature, identity history, replay, one-hop and expiry rules remain.
No claim that this caused the particular unresolved target's missing answer.
