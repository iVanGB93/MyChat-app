Encrypted relay-block streaming — local implementation, not deployed

Scope
Negotiated relayBlockStream:1 on the existing authenticated WSS connection.
Both put-batch ciphertext uploads and get-batch ciphertext responses use bounded
records. No additional socket, no decryption/re-encryption, no destination keys
at the custodian, no changes to signed v4 manifests or 3,000,000-byte blocks.
Peers without the capability retain their existing bounded bulk transport.

Contract
8,000 base64 characters per record; eight-record sender credit window, credit
every four records. Two incoming and two outgoing bounded attempts per session,
15-second attempt deadlines, exact offset/order and fixed header validation.
Bounded 30-second late-frame tombstones avoid tearing down the shared connection
on one timed-out transfer. Existing repeated-media-timeout policy remains intact.
Only outstanding bulk requests admit streamed responses; uploads require the
existing upload capability and inbound handler budget. Authentication/current
session is checked for every frame. Partial attempt buffers are not persisted.
Existing offer/quota, signature, atomic custody transaction, received prefix,
idempotency, recipient receipt and file-publication logic remains authoritative.
Transport credit is not storage, delivery or read confirmation. Storage replies
remain generated only by the existing verified transaction after commit.

Limitations
This is transport streaming, not incremental disk/crypto preparation. Existing
batch encoding and one complete bounded ciphertext block are still materialized
for verification/storage. Do not claim reduced preparation time or internet speed
improvement from these tests. No live AoN changed or new deployment authorized.
