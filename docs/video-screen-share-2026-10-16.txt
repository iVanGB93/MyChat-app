Video layout and screen-sharing phone verification (October 16 local)

Observed baseline
- Remote camera video always used contain, leaving unused space.
- Screen capture did reach the other Dev phone, including the home screen.
  A blanket transport failure was not reproduced. Sharing while the full call
  view remained visible produced confusing repeated video/controls.
- react-native-webrtc RTCRtpSender.replaceTrack catches native rejection and
  returns without updating its JS track, allowing our previous code to claim
  sharing even after that failure path.

Changes
- Camera video fills the call stage; shared display uses contain to preserve
  the complete screen. Preview mirrors camera only.
- A small ordered WebRTC data channel carries versioned presentation hints,
  over the existing authenticated call connection. No AoN protocol/deploy needed.
  Bounded hints cannot authorize calls, alter identity or trigger capture.
  Older peers without hints remain compatible with the call, but cannot
  automatically select the shared-screen fit mode.
- Successful capture minimizes the sender's call into the existing app overlay.
  Receiver hides its own controls/self-view initially so content is visible;
  tapping the received screen brings controls back.
- Track replacement verifies sender track ID before declaring sharing active.
  A swallowed native rejection releases capture and leaves camera enabled.

Verification
- Both com.axonic.dev phones used; official apps unchanged.
- Baseline and updated real video calls; screen capture sent in both directions.
- Receiver decoded 1440x3120 shared display frames. Sender's encoded frame count
  advanced 403 to 604 and receiver decoded count 3874 to 4076 across the final
  home-screen check. Receiver screenshot confirmed home screen, not just a status.
- Cancel capture: call remained connected with camera frames flowing.
- Stop share on second phone: first phone returned from 1440x3120 screen frames
  to 1280x720 camera frames, without ending the call.
- Calls ended, capture released, temporary JS peer-connection probes restored.
- Full check passed (1209 pass, 3 skipped) before two additional targeted tests.
  Final focused suite: 22 passed, including camera/screen fit and invalid hints.
- No production build, installation, push or deployment performed.
