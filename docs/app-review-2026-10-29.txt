Fixed in this review
1. Transfer progress could invalidate pending-selection hydration repeatedly. Separate job and selection revisions now publish valid selections without overwriting newer job progress. Regression covers progress arriving during an in-flight read.
2. Camera close/background could race with capture resolution before unmount. Dismissal invalidates capture immediately; late results are ignored. Tests cover close, background, normal capture and duplicate taps.
3. Quote navigation could repeat on receipts/history updates and keep resetting the highlight timeout. A target is positioned once; explicit failed-index retries remain supported. Tests cover updates and retry behavior.

Earlier scrolling fix retained
Automatic positioning yields to touch/drag/momentum; scroll events themselves do not create correction loops. Voice seeking claims horizontal drags only. Reply swipes yield to vertical movement. Those changes are also after the existing AAB.
