Call audio output selector — October 13, 2026

Voice calls: Audio button. Video calls: Options > Audio output.
Choices are Automatic, Phone, Speaker, and currently available communication
devices (Bluetooth, wired headphones, USB audio). Native device names distinguish
headsets; disconnected devices disappear. The actual output is marked In use.

Automatic captures the anticipated media output before entering communication
mode on Android 13+, maps a media Bluetooth route to its communication counterpart,
and prefers the current/available headset. An active media speaker is preserved.
Without an external route, voice defaults to Phone and video to Speaker.
Explicit choices persist through new connections; disconnection resets Automatic.
Android 12+ uses communication-device selection and callbacks. Older Android uses
speaker/SCO routing with one choice per device type, not per Bluetooth headset.

Audio ownership lasts for the mounted media call, including in-app minimization
and system PiP. Ending/unmounting restores routing and the previous audio mode.
Native startup/selection/restoration are serialized; asynchronous native route
events update the display without pretending that a pending switch already took
effect. Existing development clients report that the native build needs updating.
