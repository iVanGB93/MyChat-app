Video-call hangup delivery — October 12, 2026

Hangup was durably saved, then media closed while the signed terminal event
waited for periodic direct/relay delivery. The peer could therefore enter ICE
recovery before learning that the other participant intentionally ended the call.

The coordinator now notifies composition after persisting end/cancel/reject/busy.
Composition immediately starts direct draining and an independent, bounded relay
deposit. The relay deposit bypasses the periodic cooldown and any stalled poll,
targets the selected participant for confirmed calls, and sends only a terminal
event from the verified durable outbox. Deposits do not substitute for signed
recipient receipts; failed or unacknowledged delivery remains eligible for retry.
Local hangup does not await network delivery. Genuine connectivity recovery is
unchanged. No control signature, wire format, admission rule or deadline changed.
