Call setup: clock-skew retry fix, 2026-10-14

Finding
The Dev phone clock was approximately two seconds ahead of the emulator.
Call-control verification permits events up to five seconds in the future, but
receipt generation used the receiver's earlier wall time. Its own verification
then rejected the receipt (issuedAt < event.issuedAt), after durable admission.
This prevented observation of the initial invitation and delayed the selected
receipt until a periodic retry after the receiving clock caught up.
Immediate ringing/acceptance could likewise predate the invitation.

Change
Receipt issuance now uses max(local time, admitted event issuance).
Coordinator replies use max(local time, invitation issuance).
Expiry remains based on actual local time and the original invitation/event
and record bounds. No signature, identity, replay, selection-ACK, five-second
future tolerance, or durable-state validation was relaxed. No wire change.
Changes are in the mobile source; no AoN deployment or production build here.
