Event-driven avatar synchronization — 2026-10-14 UTC

Implemented
- Authenticated connection events, avatar publication and contact/group sharing
  policy changes trigger synchronization. Removed the periodic avatar recovery
  tick from the mobile and hosted runtime loops.
- A negotiated profileOffers capability enables encrypted signed-manifest
  announcements. Hash/revision/size/signature are exchanged before image bytes.
- Matching images advance their signed revision without requesting the image.
  Signed zero-byte removals clear cached avatars without an attachment transfer.
- Changed/missing images create a durable request bound to the offered revision
  and hash. Obsolete requests cannot trigger transfer of a newer version.
- Ordinary encrypted image chunks, signatures, hashes, size/dimension limits,
  receipts and retry persistence are preserved. A recovered/lost partial cache
  can request all parts again. No avatar messages appear in conversation history.
- Only eligible contacts and group members participate. Unknown routing peers,
  blocked peers and hidden direct conversations remain excluded as before.
- Failed announcements retry at most three times per event; no permanent poll.
  Successful unchanged announcements are suppressed until reconnection/change.
- Older clients retain compatible request/image handling through negotiation.
  Production phone installations were not replaced and need a later app release
  to obtain the new mobile behavior.

debugger polling and are not precise wire latencies.

Offline/restart: emulator-5556 was closed, the sender changed its avatar, and
the recipient was reopened without reinstalling or clearing data. It received
the new avatar automatically. No image was queued for it while offline.
Cold startup/network reconnection plus observation took 67.6 seconds; that
remaining reconnection latency is not solved by this avatar optimization.
