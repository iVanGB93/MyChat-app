Whole-app floating style audit — 2026-10-28

Design contract
- Floating navigation and modal headers: 28dp corners, 12dp gutters, thin cyan outline, restrained shadow.
- Cards: 24dp corners; form/action controls: 22dp corners. Central Chrome tokens in src/theme/index.ts.
- Social, private/group conversations and Profile allow content behind floating navigation with explicit scroll clearance.
- Functional media geometry is separate: QR quiet zones, video surface clipping, photo aspect ratios, message/reply geometry, scan targets and native Android system surfaces are intentionally retained.
- Buttons retain semantic danger/disabled/selected states and existing behavior. No networking, permissions or storage behavior changes.
