Attachment source readiness and recovery — 2026-10-14

Finding
The incoming worker treated an explicit unavailable response or absent route
response like malformed attachment data. Its one-second first job retry was
overridden by a separate ten-second source exclusion. A now-ready source could
therefore remain unused for nine additional seconds.

Change
Classify absent responses and explicit unavailable download replies as temporary
source unavailability. Align that source exclusion with the existing exponential
job backoff (1, 2, 4, 8, 16, then 30 seconds). Successful verified progress resets
failures as before. Malformed responses/proofs retain a minimum ten-second
exclusion. Preserve verified prefixes, cancellation, final hash and signed receipt.
No protocol or encryption changes; no AoN deployment or native rebuild needed.

boundaries and consolidation passed. git diff --check passed.
New deterministic coverage: transient empty/unavailable replies, persistent
unavailability backoff, malformed response exclusion, and partial bulk-download
resumption after temporary unavailability.
