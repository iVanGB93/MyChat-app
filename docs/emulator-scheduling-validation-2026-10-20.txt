- Real A-to-B text: delivered/read, delivery observed at 3,211 ms.

- Final attachment-worker snapshots on both devices: not running, no
  scheduled timer, zero failures. A snapshot is not a long idle-duration test.

Timing caveat: observed delivery timings include debugger and polling overhead.
These results are not release-mode benchmarks or phone battery tests.
